Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

The short version: Fundfinch does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. If you buy Pro, an anonymous purchase record is kept so the subscription works.

Fundfinch is a money-skills learning app for teens and young adults ages 13–25. We designed it so that it does not collect personal information.

What we collect

No personal information. Fundfinch has:

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No bank, card, or brokerage connections
  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • No chat, social features, or contact between users
  • One optional purchase, Fundfinch Pro (see below)
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts

Fundfinch Pro purchases

Fundfinch Pro is an optional subscription (or one-time lifetime purchase). Apple or Google handles the payment, and we never see your name, email or payment details.

To know which devices have Pro, we use RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat only starts when you open the Pro page or tap Restore purchases, or on a device that already has Pro. It then keeps an anonymous purchase record: a random ID created by the app, the product bought, and the purchase and renewal dates. It receives no name, email, ad ID or anything you enter in Fundfinch. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

What stays on your device

The nickname, age, and progress (points, completed lessons, and rewards) you enter are saved only on your device so the app remembers where you left off. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

You can erase it at any time by tapping Reset all progress in Settings, or by deleting the app.

App updates

To deliver new lessons and fixes, Fundfinch checks our update service (Expo EAS Update) when it opens. Each check sends a random install ID created by the app (not linked to your name or any account), the app and system version, and your IP address, which every internet request reveals. If an update fails to load, the error message is sent once so we can fix it. Expo keeps these records as our service provider and doesn’t use them for advertising.

Not financial advice

Lessons are general education, not personalized financial, tax, legal, or insurance advice. Rules and dollar limits change over time; check official sources before making decisions.

Age

Fundfinch is made for ages 13 to 25. It isn’t meant for children under 13; for younger learners, see our kids app, Dimefinch. Because Fundfinch doesn’t collect personal information, the same protections apply at every age. If you’re under 18, ask a parent or guardian before buying Fundfinch Pro.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Fundfinch handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
