Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The short version: Fundfinch does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Fundfinch is a money-skills learning app for young adults ages 18–25. We designed it so that it does not collect personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Fundfinch has:

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No bank, card, or brokerage connections
  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No chat, social features, or contact between users
  • No in-app purchases (coins are play money and have no real-world value)
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts

What stays on your device

The nickname, age, and progress (coins, completed lessons, and items) you enter are saved only on your device so the app remembers where you left off. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

You can erase it at any time by tapping Reset all progress in Settings, or by deleting the app.

App updates

Fundfinch may download new lessons and fixes from our update service (Expo EAS Update). These downloads do not include any personal information about you. As with any internet request, the update service may briefly process technical information such as an IP address in order to deliver the download, but it is not used to identify or track you.

Not financial advice

Lessons are general education, not personalized financial, tax, legal, or insurance advice. Rules and dollar limits change over time; check official sources before making decisions.

Age

Fundfinch is intended for people 18 and older. For younger learners, see our kids app, Dimefinch.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Fundfinch handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email support@fundfinch.app.
