How it works

Learn it, check it, practice it.

Each lesson takes about three minutes. No jargon left unexplained, and no feel-good tips without the math behind them.

01

Learn with real numbers

Short cards explain one idea at a time, each with an “In practice” example.

In practice$3,000 at 24% APR, paying only the minimum, takes roughly 12 years and over $7,400 total.
02

Check your understanding

Scenario questions, not trivia. Every answer comes with the reasoning, so a wrong pick still teaches you something.

03

Practice with play money

Earn points, keep them in a practice account that pays daily compound interest, and save toward milestones like a first apartment or an emergency fund.
